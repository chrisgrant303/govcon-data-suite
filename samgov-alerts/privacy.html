Privacy Policy: Contract Opportunity Alerts for SAM.gov (Unofficial) (Chrome extension)

Effective date: October 15, 2026

Contract Opportunity Alerts for SAM.gov (Unofficial) ("the Extension") is published by CCE Partners LLC ("we", "us"). Contact: chris@hichrisgrant.com.

Summary

The Extension runs entirely in your browser. We do not operate any server for it and we do not collect, receive, store, sell or share any of your data. The only service the Extension contacts is the official SAM.gov API at https://api.sam.gov, using the API key you provide.

What the Extension stores on your device

Using Chrome's extension storage (chrome.storage.local, which stays on your device and is not synced):

  • Your SAM.gov API key, which you paste in Options, and the date you saved it (to remind you that SAM.gov asks for keys to be renewed every 90 days). The key is used only to authenticate your requests to api.sam.gov.
  • Your filters and settings: keywords, NAICS codes, set-asides, notice types, agency text, state, check interval, your key's daily limit, and whether desktop notifications are on.
  • Matching notices and a "seen" list: notice IDs and public notice details (title, solicitation number, notice type, agency and its full office path, posted date, response deadline, set-aside, NAICS, place-of-performance state, link) for notices that matched your filters, and when you last opened the list, so it can tell you which ones are new.
  • A request log and check status: timestamps of API requests in the last 24 hours (to keep you under SAM.gov's daily limit), the time and result of the last check, and which posting dates have already been read for your current filters (so a check can continue where the last one stopped).

You can delete all of this at any time with Delete all extension data in Options, which first stops any check that is running, then erases everything the Extension stored, stops scheduled checks and gives back the notifications permission. Uninstalling the Extension also deletes it. ("Remove key" and "Clear seen notices" in Options delete only those items.)

What is sent, and to whom

  • To SAM.gov (api.sam.gov) only: your API key and your search parameters (posted-date range, notice types, and where applicable a NAICS code, a set-aside code, a state and a keyword; when you watch several keywords or NAICS codes, each is sent in its own request), when a scheduled or manual check runs. SAM.gov's handling of these requests is governed by SAM.gov's own terms and privacy policy (https://sam.gov/about/terms-of-use). SAM.gov states that API calls made with your key may be monitored.
  • To us: nothing. No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising or tracking.
  • Links you click (such as a notice on sam.gov) open in a normal browser tab and are governed by those sites' policies.

Personal information in SAM.gov data

SAM.gov API results can include contracting officers' names, emails and phone numbers. The Extension discards these fields when it reads the results; they are never stored or displayed.

Permissions

  • storage: keep the data listed above on your device.
  • alarms: run checks on your schedule.
  • Access to https://api.sam.gov/*: call the SAM.gov Opportunities API.
  • notifications (optional): only if you turn on desktop notifications.

What we do not do

We do not sell or transfer user data to third parties, do not use it for any purpose other than the Extension's single purpose, and do not use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

Children

The Extension is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13.

Not affiliated with SAM.gov

The Extension is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by SAM.gov, the U.S. General Services Administration or the U.S. Government.
